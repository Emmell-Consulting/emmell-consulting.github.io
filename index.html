I am Martin Leven, founder of Emmell Consulting.

Policing organisations, public-sector bodies, consultancy firms and industry partners often face difficult decisions involving reform, technology, transformation and organisational change. In those situations, independent advice, constructive challenge and practical experience can help turn strategic ambition into successful delivery.

My advice draws on experience gained through the creation of Police Scotland, founding and chairing the National Police Technology Council, leadership of the National Policing Digital Strategy, and more than 35 years of technology and transformation leadership across multiple sectors and international markets.

Today, I support organisations reviewing strategy, improving digital capability, planning major change or testing important assumptions before significant decisions are made. My focus is on helping clients make informed decisions, identify practical next steps and deliver meaningful outcomes.

About Martin

Following my retirement from the Police Digital Service, I established Emmell Consulting to undertake selected assignments that are interesting, worthwhile and involve working with good people.

After more than 35 years of technology and transformation leadership, I continue to enjoy helping organisations think through complex challenges without the constraints of a full-time executive role. I am fortunate to be at a stage in my career where I can be selective about the work I take on, focusing on assignments where experience, independent thinking and practical advice can genuinely add value.

While policing has been a significant part of my career, my experience spans multiple sectors and international markets. I have worked across biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, contact centres, technology-enabled services and policing, supporting organisations and leadership teams throughout the UK, Europe, North America, Africa and Asia-Pacific. That breadth enables me to bring ideas, lessons and perspectives from different organisations, sectors and operating environments to the challenges my clients face today.

Experience behind the advice

  • 2022–2025
    Director of Strategy and Transformation, Police Digital Service
  • Police Scotland
    Director of ICT, following the service's creation
  • SPSA
    Chief Information Officer, Scottish Police Services Authority
  • Founder
    National Police Technology Council, founding chair

As Chief Information Officer at the Scottish Police Services Authority, I was responsible for the technology requirements associated with establishing the new national police service before becoming Director of ICT within Police Scotland. During this period, I also founded and chaired the National Police Technology Council, bringing together technology leaders from across UK policing to share learning, address common challenges and support greater collaboration on national technology priorities.

From 2022 to 2025, I served as Director of Strategy and Transformation at the Police Digital Service, leading the development and publication of the National Policing Digital Strategy while overseeing innovation and professional services.

Alongside policing leadership, my wider career has involved working with scientists, business leaders, government stakeholders and technology suppliers across multiple industries and international markets. Those experiences continue to shape my approach to strategy, leadership and organisational change.

I have also spoken at major international conferences, including the World Policing Summit in Dubai, and given evidence to committees of the Scottish Parliament on numerous occasions. These opportunities have reinforced the importance of communicating complex issues clearly, responding constructively to scrutiny and challenge, and helping organisations make confident, well-informed decisions in demanding environments.
